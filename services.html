Services

Full-lifecycle water engineering, from model to permit.

Four disciplines, one team. We move between hydrology, drainage, irrigation and treatment on the same project so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

01

Hydraulic & Hydrology Modeling

We build the models that everything else depends on: how water moves across a watershed, through a channel, or into a pipe network — and what happens when the design storm hits. Floodplain & FEMA-compliant studies, watershed & stormwater analysis (HEC-RAS, HEC-HMS), channel & culvert capacity analysis.

02

Drainage Design

Grading and drainage that hold up under the actual storm, not just the review checklist — sized, sequenced and detailed for construction. Site grading & storm drain networks, detention & retention design, erosion & sediment control plans.

03

Irrigation Systems

Irrigation design for growers and landscapes that need every gallon accounted for — from the pump station to the last emitter. Agricultural & landscape irrigation design, pump station sizing & controls, water rights & allocation compliance.

04

Water & Wastewater Treatment

Process engineering for new treatment capacity and upgrades to existing plants, carried through permitting. Treatment plant design & process engineering, NPDES permitting, capacity upgrades & plant optimization.
