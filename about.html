About

Engineers who show up for the whole project.

Hydro Logic Solution was founded on a simple frustration: too many water projects get handed between specialists who never talk to each other. We keep hydrology, drainage, irrigation and treatment under one roof, on one team.

Where we're starting

Right now, that means building in public.

Before we take on client work, we're putting the tools we'd use on a real project out in the open, free, for anyone to learn from — the same Manning's equation, Rational Method and MSMA2 design chain a real drainage study runs on. If you can follow the labs, you can follow our thinking on your project.
