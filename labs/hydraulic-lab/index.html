Simulation assumptions

The virtual flume uses a fixed illustrative n=0.010 and Manning uniform-flow depth. The experiment estimates n back from virtual measurements. Matching this value verifies the calculation, not the physical model.

The apparatus is an approximate S6-style reconstruction, not an exact manufacturer CAD model. Glass panels, frame, pump and point gauges are visual; no pump curve, pipe network, turbulent flow, manometer or transient stabilization solver is included. Manually entered observations do not alter the predicted 3D water surface.
